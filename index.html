About Us


Who We Are

Rahmsuk-Nig Ltd is a trusted provider of solar energy solutions and electrical services across Nigeria. We specialize in delivering reliable and affordable solar power systems for homes, businesses, and industrial facilities.

Our team is committed to providing high-quality solar products including solar panels, batteries, inverters, and complete solar installation services tailored to meet the energy needs of our customers.

Leadership

Meet the founder leading Rahmsuk-Nig Ltd

Rahmsuk

Founder & CEO

Rahmsuk is the founder of Rahmsuk-Nig Ltd, a company focused on delivering reliable solar energy solutions and professional electrical installations. He has led multiple solar projects for residential and commercial clients, helping businesses and households transition to affordable renewable energy. With a strong commitment to innovation and sustainability, Rahmsuk continues to promote the use of clean energy technologies that improve energy efficiency and reduce dependence on traditional power sources. Through his leadership, the company strives to provide high-quality services
